The actual work

My work in the
Harbin Keyroom

Operations Specialist, Georgetown Residential Living
May to September 2025

01

Managing the shift

I supervised 4 to 6 inspectors and 2 to 4 keyroom helpers per shift. I assigned their work, checked completion and quality, and followed up on incorrect inspection reports.

02

Building the dashboards

I built Next.js dashboards backed by Supabase for approximately 24 housing staff. They brought together group arrival schedules, failed inspections, work orders, and keyroom visits.

I wrote custom connectors to download Google Sheets responses as CSV files and import them into Supabase. The largest table held more than 20,000 inspection records from a Google Forms workflow. The housing data covered more than 3,000 living spaces.

03

Tracking keys

I built a key-tracking application with Google sign-in and controlled staff access through Supabase. When a key went missing, I used the logs to find a problem with how staff exchanged keys. We changed the lock and updated the written handoff procedure.

↳

How I built the software
I used AI to help write the code. I decided what staff needed to track, connected the inspection data, and built the dashboard views around our daily work.
