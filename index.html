The actual work

Responsibility first.
Tools where they helped.

Operations Specialist, Georgetown Residential Living
May–September 2025

01

Coordinate the people

I supervised 4–6 inspectors and 2–4 keyroom helpers per shift: assigning tasks, checking completion and quality, and following up when reporting wasn’t right.

02

Make the operation visible

I built Next.js dashboards backed by Supabase for approximately 24 housing staff. They brought together group arrival schedules, failed inspections, work orders, and keyroom visits.

Custom CSV connectors consolidated 20,000+ inspection records from a Google Forms and Sheets workflow. The housing data covered 3,000+ living spaces.

03

Make responsibility clearer

I built a key-tracking application with Google authentication and selective user access. When a key went missing, tracing activity logs revealed conflicting instructions between teams and informed changes to the handoff procedure and SOP.

↳

How I built the software
I used AI to help write the code. My contribution was identifying the operational need, defining the workflows, connecting the data, and putting the tools in the context of the team’s work.
